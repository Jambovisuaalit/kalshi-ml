# Binance BTCUSDT spot proxy OOS validation

Training: December 2024. OOS prediction: December 2025. Contract outcomes: official Kalshi December 30–31, 2025 only for evaluation. 2025 outcomes never enter fitting.

**Strict limitations**: Binance minute-open is not an official Kalshi strike. Historical Binance bar availability (+1 second) is a working assumption, not a verified feed arrival time. Historical Kalshi minute-candle OHLC prices are not executable order-book quotes. This research cannot establish live trading profitability. A later, genuinely untouched Kalshi holdout and official strike source are required.
